DESCRIPTION: Description Centrifugal pump critical cavitation point detection device and detection method Technical field The invention belongs to cavitation performance of centrifugal pump experimental technique fields, and in particular to a kind of inspection of centrifugal pump critical cavitation point Survey device and detection method. Background technique Centrifugal pump during the work time, often because pump in fluid local pressure decline due to generate cavitation phenomenons.Cavitation Generated shock wave has detrimental effect to the component or even pump case of centrifugal pump, and will lead to centrifugal pump when serious cannot normal work Make.For this reason, it is necessary to determine the critical cavitation point of centrifugal pump by cavitation experiment, that is, critical NPSH. Currently, determine that the common cavitation experiment method of critical cavitation point is realized by manual test mode, it is substantially former Reason is to change inlet pressure level by increasing the resistance on centrifugal pump inlet pipeline, while by changing the resistance on outlet pipeline Power is to keep flow constant, so that constantly NPSH value (net positive suction head value) is reduced, until lift decline 3%, NPSH value at this time Net positive suction head value as to be measured.Since the net positive suction head of centrifugal pump can not also accurately calculate cavitation experiment by theory at present Difficulty with higher again itself, this allows for often occurring in real work net positive suction head measurement inaccuracy, and the testing time is longer And labor intensity it is larger the problems such as. For disadvantages mentioned above, researchers propose relevant solution: being on the one hand that cavitation occurs according to centrifugal pump When relevant parameter the characteristics of, propose using Noise Method, vibratory drilling method, pressure pulsation method, electrical measuring method, image method etc. judge from The method of heart pump cavitation situation, and obtained certain application；It on the other hand is that Cavitation Occurred in Centrifugal Pumps examination is modified by designed, designed Experiment device increases some accessories and test component, to improve measurement accuracy.However, due to technology or economic aspect Limitation, the application of these solutions in practical projects is few, and operating process is inconvenient, is difficult to realize automation survey Examination. Summary of the invention It is an object of the invention to overcome, net positive suction head measurement accuracy present in existing cavitation experiment method is not high, surveys Try that the time is longer and the biggish defect of labor intensity. To achieve the goals above, present invention firstly provides a kind of centrifugal pump critical cavitation point detection devices, including under State component: (1) imported valve and inlet pressure transmitter being connected on inlet pipeline； (2) outlet valve, flowmeter and the outlet pressure transmitter being connected on outlet pipeline； (3) torque rotary speed sensor being connected between motor and centrifugal pump to be measured； (4) simultaneously sampling multi-function card and industrial personal computer； The imported valve, inlet pressure transmitter, outlet valve, flowmeter, outlet pressure transmitter and torque rotary speed Sensor is connect by signal wire with simultaneously sampling multi-function card, and simultaneously sampling multi-function card passes through Ethernet and industrial personal computer Connection. Further, the imported valve and outlet valve are pneumatic control valve door. Above-mentioned detection device provides a kind of tool to achieve the purpose of the present invention, by using above-mentioned detection device, originally Invention further provides a kind of detection method of centrifugal pump critical cavitation point comprising following step: Step 1 records the outlet valve of cavitation experiment flow rate working conditions point to be measured during performance of centrifugal pump test Opening value, while when critical cavitation is estimated according to the net positive suction head value on centrifugal pump nameplate corresponding inlet pressure range； Step 2 opens imported valve entirely, and what is recorded when setting performance test for the opening value of outlet valve opens Angle value starts the measurement of first group of data； Step 3, checks whether the deviation of centrifugal pump actual flow value and target value flow value is being adjusted in error range, If not being finely adjusted in the adjusting error range to outlet valve； Step 4, after inlet pressure transmitter, outlet pressure transmitter, meter readings are stablized, acquisition inlet pressure, Outlet pressure, flow, revolving speed, performance number, and calculate lift, net positive suction head value； Step 5, judges whether lift declines 3%, and condition meets, then off-test；Condition is unsatisfactory for, then is judged at this time Inlet pressure size, to determine the amplitude that turns down imported valve； Step 6, if inlet pressure is greater than corresponding inlet pressure when the critical cavitation estimated when executing step 1 The upper limit of range then carries out coarse adjustment to imported valve (amplitude that imported valve turns down is larger)；If inlet pressure falls into execution When the critical cavitation estimated when step 1 within the scope of corresponding inlet pressure, then imported valve is finely adjusted (into The amplitude that mouth valve turns down is small). Further, step 3 is repeated to step 6, until centrifugal pump lift decline 3%. The present invention have it is following the utility model has the advantages that Due to the detection device in the present invention use imported valve, inlet pressure transmitter, outlet valve, flowmeter, The components such as outlet pressure transmitter, torque rotary speed sensor, simultaneously sampling multi-function card and industrial personal computer, and the side in the present invention Method carries out coarse adjustment or fine tuning to imported valve and outlet valve through reasonable set target value and in a manner of automatic control, from And the stability of test macro and the degree of regulation of critical cavitation point are improved, it is effectively shortened the testing time.In short, this hair Bright automatization level, measuring accuracy and the efficiency that can increase substantially the detection of centrifugal pump critical cavitation point.
