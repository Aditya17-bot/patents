ABSTRACT: Abstract The invention discloses a method and a system for detecting a water pump cavitation residual value, wherein an automatic acquisition mode is utilized to control data acquisition of the whole detection process, when application information acquired by manual control is received, the manual control mode is given the authority to acquire cavitation early-stage data, and the acquisition of cavitation numerical values in the automatic acquisition process is temporarily stopped; after the collection of the data points at the early stage of the cavitation is finished, the authority of the data at the early stage of the cavitation is recovered, and whether the cavitation occurs is judged according to the condition of the data at the early stage of the cavitation and compared with a critical cavitation value; when the water pump cavitation reaches the critical cavitation value, the automatic acquisition mode is utilized to automatically complete the acquisition and calculation of the water pump cavitation value, and the accurate numerical value of the water pump cavitation allowance is obtained.

DESCRIPTION: Description Translated from Chinese 水泵汽蚀余量值的检测方法与系统 Method and system for detecting NPSH value of water pump 技术领域 technical field 本发明涉及一种水泵汽蚀余量值的检测方法与系统。 The invention relates to a method and a system for detecting the NPSH value of a water pump. 背景技术 Background technique 汽蚀余量是水泵的一项重要技术指标，它涉及到水泵产品的性能及使用寿命等，因此准确测试出该指标对于生产企业及用户都具有重大意义。不同类型、结构的水泵发生汽蚀现象即达到临界汽蚀值时是没有规律的，即便是相同型号的水泵，每台泵的汽蚀余量值也不完全相同，目前测试中虽然普遍利用了微机采集数据的手段，但由于水泵的汽蚀现象具有一种特性，即水泵在即将达到临界汽蚀余量值时，该汽蚀值由缓慢变化突然转为持续、快速的变化直至临界汽蚀值(该值为水泵的汽蚀指标值)，且在达到该临界汽蚀值后汽蚀值仍继续快速向下变化，同时水泵在达到临界汽蚀值时水泵并无明显的外部特征变化，如流量、压力、运转状态等均没有明显变化，以上因素造成了测试人员在检测过程中只能凭借以往的经验去控制微机采集相关数据，这样往往造成检测的数据结果与真实汽蚀值有着较大出入，即汽蚀试验受操作者的人为因素影响很大，如熟练程度、控制顺序、对参数变化的操控能力等因素，且严重影响了工作效率。 The NPSH is an important technical indicator of the water pump, which relates to the performance and service life of the water pump, so it is of great significance to both manufacturers and users to accurately test this indicator. The cavitation phenomenon of different types and structures of water pumps is irregular, that is, when the critical cavitation value is reached. Even for the same type of water pump, the NPSH value of each pump is not the same. It is a means of collecting data by a computer, but because the cavitation phenomenon of the water pump has a characteristic, that is, when the water pump is about to reach the critical NPSH value, the cavitation value suddenly changes from a slow change to a continuous and rapid change until the critical cavitation (this value is the cavitation index value of the water pump), and after reaching the critical cavitation value, the cavitation value continues to change rapidly downward, and the water pump has no obvious external characteristic changes when the water pump reaches the critical cavitation value. For example, there is no obvious change in flow rate, pressure, operating state, etc. The above factors cause testers to rely on past experience to control the computer to collect relevant data during the detection process, which often results in the detection data results and the actual cavitation value. The big discrepancy means that the cavitation test is greatly affected by the operator's human factors, such as proficiency, control sequence, control ability for parameter changes, etc., and seriously affects the work efficiency. 水泵汽蚀余量的检测有两个目的，其一是为了得到水泵的汽蚀前期过程，即水泵从正常运行到发生汽蚀的前期过程，其二是为了得到水泵的汽蚀余量值，该值是水泵产品的重要性能指标。 The detection of the NPSH of the water pump has two purposes, one is to obtain the pre-cavitation process of the water pump, that is, the early process from the normal operation of the pump to the occurrence of cavitation, and the other is to obtain the NPSH value of the water pump. This value is an important performance index of water pump products. 当前，水泵汽蚀余量的检测手段有人工控制微机采集、微机自动采集两种方式，但这两种方式都存在问题： At present, there are two methods for detecting the NPSH of water pump: manual control microcomputer acquisition and microcomputer automatic acquisition, but both methods have problems: 人工控制微机采集的方式能够保证有效检测出水泵的汽蚀前期过程，因为操作者可以根据水泵的流量、扬程主要基本参数的缓慢变化灵活确定汽蚀产生过程中的数据采集点，但在发生汽蚀时水泵并无明显的外部特征变化，且汽蚀值突然转为持续、快速的变化，人工控制采集就无法保证采集到客观、真实的汽蚀数值； The method of manual control of microcomputer acquisition can ensure the effective detection of the early process of cavitation of the pump, because the operator can flexibly determine the data collection point in the process of cavitation according to the slow change of the main basic parameters of the flow and head of the pump. When the pump is corroded, there is no obvious external characteristic change, and the cavitation value suddenly changes to a continuous and rapid change, and manual control of the acquisition cannot guarantee the acquisition of objective and real cavitation values; 微机自动采集的方式可以保证汽蚀数值的真实性，但由于不同型号的水泵，汽蚀性能千差万别，即使同型号、同批次的水泵因为原材料、加工精度、加工工艺等因素的影响，汽蚀性能也有着较大差别，即发生汽蚀的过程是没有规律的，微机自动采集方式只能按照固定的程序按固定间隔采集汽蚀前期过程的数据点，所以无法得出科学、全面的汽蚀过程曲线。 The method of automatic collection by microcomputer can ensure the authenticity of cavitation value, but due to the different types of water pumps, the cavitation performance varies widely, even if the same type and batch of water pumps are affected by raw materials, processing accuracy, processing technology and other factors, cavitation There is also a big difference in performance, that is, the process of cavitation is irregular, and the automatic collection method of microcomputer can only collect data points of the pre-cavitation process at fixed intervals according to a fixed program, so it is impossible to obtain a scientific and comprehensive cavitation. process curve. 发明内容 SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION 本发明为了解决上述问题，提出了一种水泵汽蚀余量值的检测方法与系统，本发明不仅能大幅降低对测试人员的技术要求，而且还能有效保证测试值的准确性、科学性、真实性，同时极大提高了检测效率，从而为有效保障水泵产品质量提供了科学的数据基础。 In order to solve the above problems, the present invention proposes a method and system for detecting the NPSH value of a water pump. The manual control collection function and the computer automatic collection function are organically integrated by the hierarchical decentralization system. Microcomputer automatic acquisition has control rights and cavitation value acquisition application rights, and control rights always run through the entire test process; manual control microcomputer acquisition does not have control rights and application rights, but only has application rights, and the test operator manually clicks to execute application rights, control The right party automatically grants manual control the 'pre-cavitation data collection and application right', and temporarily waives the 'cavitation numerical acquisition and application right', and instantly completes the collection of the pre-cavitation data point. After the point collection is completed, the control party will immediately The application right is automatically recovered, so that the operator can collect data according to the flexible judgment of the previous cavitation data, and when the water pump cavitation reaches the critical cavitation value, it has the automatic collection function of 'the right to collect and apply the cavitation value'. The collection and calculation of the cavitation value of the water pump can be automatically completed in an instant, and the accurate value of the cavitation allowance of the water pump can be obtained. The invention scheme integrates control rights, two types of application rights, and application rights throughout the entire test process, realizes the perfect and efficient integration of manual control functions and computer automatic functions, and ensures the scientificity and authenticity of test results. 本发明并不是将以上两种检测手段进行简单的组合或堆砌，即A+B模式，因为这种组合模式从以下几个方面分析是不可行的。首先，从法律法规角度分析，中华人民共和国产品质量法及水泵产品的国家标准等法律法规不允许在进行产品质量检测时，对同一个产品的同一个性能指标进行两次检验，然后摘取两次检验的部分数据组合成一个新的数据作为质量检测的结果；其次，从数据角度分析，人工控制采集得出的前期数据与微机自动采集的汽蚀数值进行简单粗暴的拼接，无法真实反映水泵汽蚀前期过程的末端到发生汽蚀时的趋势曲线；再次，从技术角度分析，由上文所述水泵发生汽蚀时的独有特性决定了这种简单的组合模式是无法实现的，即操作者何时、何种情况下决定由A模式快速准确转到B模式；最后，从工作效率方面分析，这种简单的组合模式至少要做两次试验，严重影响了工作效率。本发明是将两种采集手段做到有机的完全融合，是一种新的检测手段。 The present invention does not simply combine or stack the above two detection means, that is, the A+B mode, because this combination mode is infeasible to analyze from the following aspects. First of all, from the perspective of laws and regulations, laws and regulations such as the Product Quality Law of the People's Republic of China and the national standards of water pump products do not allow two inspections of the same performance index of the same product during product quality inspection, and then extract two Part of the data of the second inspection is combined into a new data as the result of the quality inspection; secondly, from the perspective of data analysis, the early data collected by manual control and the cavitation value automatically collected by the computer are simply and rudely spliced, which cannot truly reflect the water pump. The trend curve from the end of the pre-cavitation process to the occurrence of cavitation; again, from a technical point of view, the unique characteristics of the water pump when cavitation occurs above determine that this simple combination mode cannot be realized, that is, When and under what circumstances the operator decides to switch from mode A to mode B quickly and accurately; finally, from the perspective of work efficiency, this simple combination mode requires at least two tests, which seriously affects work efficiency.
