ABSTRACT: Abstract The invention discloses a closed-loop centrifugal pump cavitation allowance automatic test system and a method, which belong to the technical field of centrifugal pump test, and the technical scheme is as follows: the device comprises a gas separation tank, wherein the top of the gas separation tank is connected with a vacuum pump through a vacuum pipeline, and the vacuum pipeline is provided with a vacuum flow regulating valve; the lower part of the gas separation tank is connected with a tested pump through a first test pipeline, the tested pump is connected to the upper part of the gas separation tank through a second test pipeline, and the second test pipeline is provided with an electric flow regulating valve which is connected with a controller so as to automatically control the flow of the tested pump and the vacuumizing rate of the cavitation test process. According to the scheme, the vacuumizing rate can be set and regulated, meanwhile, the flow parameter of the tested pump is ensured to fluctuate within a standard specified range, cavitation allowance data of the centrifugal pump are automatically collected, and high test precision is ensured.

DESCRIPTION: Description Automatic test system and method for cavitation margin of closed-loop centrifugal pump Technical Field The invention belongs to the technical field of centrifugal pump testing, and particularly relates to a closed-loop centrifugal pump cavitation allowance automatic testing system and method. Background The statements herein merely provide background information related to the present disclosure and may not necessarily constitute prior art. Currently, centrifugal pump cavitation test equipment is broadly divided into three types: dry pit method, open test loop and closed test loop. The closed test loop is mainly characterized in that: the test precision is high, and the valve is not easy to interfere; the manufacturing cost is high, the connection of the inlet and the outlet of the pump is relatively troublesome, and the operation is complex. The closed test circuit is a test circuit isolated from the outside atmosphere. By changing the pressure, level or temperature, NPSH is changed without affecting the head or flow until cavitation occurs in the pump. According to the requirements of NPSH test, the flow, inlet pressure, outlet pressure, water temperature and atmospheric pressure of the centrifugal pump are measured. The inventor found that in the current cavitation residual test, when the vacuum pump pumps vacuum, the flow rate of the tested pump is reduced, and if the flow rate of the tested pump is reduced to be not within a specified deviation range, the accuracy of the cavitation test is affected, so that the flow rate of the tested pump needs to be adjusted by frequently and manually adjusting the flow rate control valve. Meanwhile, the vacuum pumping speed of the vacuum pump in the cavitation test has a large influence on the cavitation test, if the speed is too high, the flow cannot be adjusted manually in time, the flow cannot be kept in a specified deviation range, and the accuracy of the cavitation test is affected; if the speed is too slow, the test time is too long, which affects the test efficiency of the cavitation test. At present, no closed loop centrifugal pump cavitation margin automatic test system and test method for automatically controlling flow fluctuation amplitude and vacuum pump evacuation rate exist. Disclosure of Invention Aiming at the defects existing in the prior art, the invention aims to provide an automatic test system and an automatic test method for cavitation margin of a closed-loop centrifugal pump, wherein the test system can automatically control flow fluctuation amplitude and vacuum pumping rate of a vacuum pump. As a further technical scheme, the first test pipeline is provided with an inlet pressure sensor, and the second test pipeline is provided with an outlet pressure sensor; the inlet pressure sensor and the outlet pressure sensor are both connected with the controller. As a further technical scheme, an isolation valve is arranged at the joint of the first test pipeline and the gas separation tank. As a further technical scheme, the second test pipeline is provided with a flowmeter, and the flowmeter is connected with the controller. As a further technical scheme, the second test pipeline extends to the inside of the gas separation tank, and the end part of the second test pipeline is bent downwards. As a further technical solution, the second test line is also connected with a third test line, the third test line is connected to the top inside the gas separation tank, and the third test line end is connected with a nozzle for spraying the degassing liquid. In a second aspect, the invention also provides a testing method adopting the closed-loop centrifugal pump cavitation allowance testing system, which comprises the following steps: starting a tested pump, adjusting the flow of the tested pump to Q+/-0.5% Q, wherein Q is the rated flow of the tested pump, and collecting the pump parameters of a starting point; starting a vacuum pump to vacuumize, and adjusting the vacuumizing rate; Collecting the real-time flow Qi of a tested pump, comparing the real-time flow Qi with the rated flow Q, collecting cavitation test data of a pump at each interval (p 1 -p' 1 )/12 pressure changes if the Qi is within the range of 0.98Q to be less than or equal to 1.02Q, calculating the real-time lift H ', comparing the real-time lift H' with the rated lift H of the tested pump, collecting the cavitation test data when the H 'is less than or equal to (1-3%) H, continuously pumping vacuum, and collecting the last parameter when the H' is about (1-10%) H, so as to finish the cavitation test; Ending the measurement of the cavitation allowance of the tested pump, and drawing a cavitation allowance performance curve of the tested pump by taking the cavitation allowance as an abscissa and the lift as an ordinate; Wherein, p' 1 represents the inlet pressure when cavitation occurs in the pump under test; p 1 represents the inlet pressure at the beginning of the cavitation test of the pump under test. The beneficial effects of the invention are as follows: According to the automatic test system for the cavitation margin of the centrifugal pump, the vacuum flow regulating valve is arranged on the vacuumizing pipeline, and the electric flow regulating valve is arranged on the test pipeline, so that the vacuumizing speed can be automatically monitored and controlled in the cavitation test process, the flow of the centrifugal pump can be automatically regulated, and the flow is kept stable; the system has high automation degree and convenient operation, and can efficiently and accurately perform the cavitation allowance test. Detailed Description It should be noted that the following detailed description is illustrative and is intended to provide further explanation of the invention. Unless defined otherwise, all technical and scientific terms used herein have the same meaning as commonly understood by one of ordinary skill in the art to which this invention belongs. In an exemplary embodiment of the present invention, as shown in fig. 1, a closed-loop centrifugal pump cavitation residual testing system for automatically controlling testing conditions is provided, which can automatically measure the cavitation residual of a centrifugal pump. The test system comprises a gas separation tank 1, a steady flow grid 2, a spray degassing liquid nozzle 3, a vacuumizing pipeline 4, an electric flow regulating valve 5, a flowmeter 6, a vacuum meter 7, a vacuum flow regulating valve 8, a vacuum pump 9, an outlet pressure sensor 10, a tested pump 11, an inlet pressure sensor 12 and an isolation valve 13. The gas separation tank 1 is internally provided with a steady flow grid 2, and the steady flow grid 2 is used for guaranteeing stable water flow. The lower part of the gas separation tank 1 is provided with an isolation valve 13 which is communicated with a first test pipeline 14, and the isolation valve 13 is used for isolating liquid; the isolation valve 13 is arranged at the lower part of the gas separation tank 1, the first test pipeline 14 is communicated with the tested pump 11, and the isolation valve 13 is communicated with the upstream of the connecting pipeline of the tested pump 11; the first test line is also provided with an inlet pressure sensor 12 corresponding to the inlet end of the pump under test for measuring inlet pressure parameters of the pump under test. The downstream end of the pump under test 11 is also in communication with a second test line 15, the second test line 15 being further provided with an outlet pressure sensor 10 corresponding to the outlet end of the pump under test for measuring an outlet pressure parameter of the pump under test. The second test pipeline is connected back to the upper part of the gas separation tank 1, and extends into the gas separation tank 1, and the end part of the second test pipeline is bent downwards, namely, the fluid in the second test pipeline enters the gas separation tank and then is conveyed downwards. An electric flow regulating valve 5 is arranged at the joint of the second test pipeline and the gas separation tank, and the electric flow regulating valve 5 is communicated with the gas separation tank 1 and is used for regulating flow; the second test line is provided with a flow meter 6 to monitor in real time the flow parameters of the centrifugal pump under test, the electric flow regulator being located downstream of the flow meter. The electric flow regulating valve and the flowmeter are communicated with the controller. The controller can automatically control the start and stop and the opening of the electric flow regulating valve according to the flow parameters returned by the flowmeter and the flow parameter fluctuation range set by the background, so that the flow of the tested pump is ensured to fluctuate within the range of +/-2% of the measured flow. The center of the top of the gas separation tank 1 is communicated with a third test pipeline 16 through a spray degassing liquid nozzle 3, the spray degassing liquid nozzle 3 is used for cooling, and the third test pipeline is communicated with a second test pipeline. In the embodiment, two steady flow grids are arranged and are respectively positioned at the upper part and the lower part of the gas separation tank; specifically, one of the flow stabilizing grids is positioned above the second test tube, and the other flow stabilizing grid is positioned above the first test tube. The top of the gas separation tank 1 is communicated with a vacuum pump 9 through a vacuumizing pipeline 4, and the gas separation tank 1 is used for storing a medium for testing. The inlet end of the vacuum pump is connected with the vacuum flow regulating valve 8, the outlet end of the vacuum pump is communicated with the atmosphere, and the vacuum pump 9 is used for pumping vacuum to the upper part of the gas separation tank 1 so as to reduce the inlet pressure of the tested pump 11, thereby achieving the cavitation occurrence condition of the tested pump 11. The inlet end of the vacuum pump 9 is connected with a vacuum flow regulating valve 8, and the vacuum flow regulating valve 8 is used for regulating the vacuumizing speed of the vacuum pump 9. The vacuumizing pipeline 4 is provided with a vacuum gauge 7, the upstream end of the vacuum flow regulating valve 8 is connected with the vacuum gauge 7, the vacuum gauge 7 is used for displaying the vacuum degree of the gas separation tank 1, and the upstream end of the vacuum gauge 7 is connected with the gas separation tank through the vacuumizing pipeline. The inlet pressure sensor 12, the outlet pressure sensor 10, the vacuum pump 9, the electric flow regulating valve 5, the flowmeter 6, the vacuum flow regulating valve 8 and the tested pump 11 are all communicated with a controller, and the controller is communicated with a tablet personal computer. The panel computer controls the electric flow regulating valve 5, the vacuum flow regulating valve 8 and the tested pump 11 through a program by a controller, so that the flow and the vacuumizing rate of the tested pump 11 are automatically regulated, and the stability of the flow and the vacuumizing rate of the tested pump is automatically maintained; and automatically processing the collected flow, inlet pressure, outlet pressure, water temperature and atmospheric pressure data, and automatically drawing a cavitation allowance performance curve of the tested pump 11. The system sends a command to the controller through a cavitation allowance test program of the tablet personal computer, and the controller regulates and controls flow parameters of the tested pump through the flowmeter and the electric flow regulating valve. The system sends a command to the controller through a cavitation margin test program of the tablet personal computer, and the controller regulates and controls the vacuumizing speed of the vacuum pump through the inlet pressure transmitter and the vacuum flow regulating valve. The following describes the procedure of the test method using the test system, as shown in fig. 2. The panel computer controls the opening and closing and opening adjustment of the electric flow regulating valve 5 and the vacuum flow regulating valve 8 through the controller, automatically maintains the stability of the flow and the vacuumizing speed of the tested pump 11, completes the measurement of the cavitation allowance under different flow rates, and draws the cavitation allowance performance curve of the tested pump. Inputting basic parameters such as cavitation allowance, flow Q, lift H, rotating speed n and the like of the tested pump, starting a test, and starting the tested pump; starting an electric flow regulating valve, regulating the flow of the tested pump to Q+/-0.5% Q, wherein Q is the rated flow of the tested pump, and collecting the parameters of the tested pump at the starting point; Starting a vacuum pump, starting a vacuum flow regulating valve, regulating the real-time vacuumizing speed Vi to V0+/-10% V0, wherein V0 is the vacuumizing initial speed, and defining V0 as the speed of the inlet pressure change of the tested pump in unit time of the testing system, wherein the speed is expressed as follows by a formula: Wherein: p' 1 represents the inlet pressure at which cavitation occurs in the pump under test; p 1 represents the inlet pressure at the beginning of the cavitation test of the pump under test; NPSHR represents the necessary cavitation margin specified by the test pump; z D represents the NPSH reference plane to reference plane head; p amb represents atmospheric pressure; p v represents the vaporization pressure; ρ 1 represents the inlet media density; g represents local gravitational acceleration; t represents the time (typically ranging from 15 to 30 minutes) for the inlet pressure of the test pump to change from p 1 to p' 1 . In the test process, calculating a real-time vacuumizing rate Vi, comparing Vi with an initial vacuumizing rate V0, closing a vacuum flow regulating valve when the real-time vacuumizing rate Vi is larger than 1.1V0, opening a large vacuum flow regulating valve when the real-time vacuumizing rate Vi is smaller than 0.9V0, and keeping the opening of the vacuum flow regulating valve unchanged when the real-time vacuumizing rate is within a range of 0.9V0-Vi-1.1V0; Comparing the real-time flow Qi of the tested pump with the rated flow Q, judging whether the Qi is within the range of 0.98Q-1.02Q, if the Qi is less than 0.98Q, opening an electric flow regulating valve, and increasing the real-time flow Qi of the tested pump until the Qi is within the range of 0.98Q-1.02Q; if Qi is more than 1.02Q, the electric flow regulating valve is closed, and the real-time flow Qi of the tested pump is reduced until Qi is within the range of 0.98Q less than or equal to Qi less than or equal to 1.02Q; when the flow is within the range of 0.98Q-Qi-1.02Q, the opening of the electric flow regulating valve is unchanged; For each drop in pressure (p 1 -p' 1 )/12, one point of cavitation margin data (including flow, inlet pressure, outlet pressure, water temperature, and barometric pressure) is collected. Simultaneously calculating the real-time lift H', comparing the real-time lift H with (1-3%) H (H is the rated lift of the pump), and continuously collecting cavitation allowance data of the tested pump at each interval (p 1 -p' 1 )/12 pressure change if H > (1-3%) H; if H 'is less than or equal to (1-3%) H, cavitation occurs, cavitation point test data are collected, vacuum is continuously extracted, and when H' is less than or equal to (1-10%) H, final cavitation allowance data are collected, so that cavitation test is completed; and (3) finishing the measurement of the cavitation allowance of the tested pump, and drawing a cavitation allowance performance curve of the tested pump by taking the cavitation allowance as an abscissa and the lift as an ordinate, wherein the cavitation test performance curve is shown in figure 3.
