Images Landscapes Emergency Alarm Devices ( AREA ) Abstract The invention discloses a gas-leakage emergency protection device. The gas-leakage emergency protection device comprises a sensor, an alarm, an alarm lamp, a fan and a programmable logic controller (PLC), wherein the sensor is used for testing the content of carbon monoxide in air, and transmitting the tested data to the PLC; the alarm is used for giving an alarm in an emergency; the alarm lamp is used for cautioning in the emergency; the fan receives an instruction of the PLC, and discharges the indoor gas in an unexpected condition; and the PLC is used for controlling the start and shutdown of the sensor, the alarm, the alarm lamp and the fan. The gas-leakage emergency protection device disclosed by the invention adopts an active defense measure and has the advantages that the reliability is high and safety problems caused by household gas leakages can be effectively avoided. Description The gas leakage emergency protecting equipment Technical field The present invention relates to a kind of gas protection device of civilian security fields, specifically, what relate to is a kind of gas leak emergency protecting equipment. Background technology Coal gas has accessed to most families, and the safety problem of coal gas aspect also is much accounted of day by day, estimates according to the expert; Family gas leaks; Can poison if airborne carbon monoxide content reaches 0.04%～0.06% o'clock people, reach at 12.5% o'clock, also possibly produce blast with air mixed. The main cause of gas poisoning: (1) in airtight room, use coal stove for heating, cook because improper ventilation, oxygen supply is insufficient, can produce a large amount of carbon monoxide savings indoor. 1. door and window closes, and does not have venting practice again, does not install or install incorrectly wind scoop. 2. careless and inadvertent, the thought paralysis causes coal gas to overflow in a large number. 3. the chimney installation is unreasonable, and nozzle flows backwards coal gas over against the air port. 4. weather conditions are bad, as blow, snow, cloudy day, air pressure be low, coal gas is difficult to circulation and discharges. (2) the city resident uses piped gas, and its CO content is 25%～30%.If pipeline leaks gas, switch is not tight, or after flame was put out in hotting plate, coal gas overflowed in a large number, can cause poisoning. (3) use gas heater, improper ventilation, bathing overlong time. (4) in the garage, start up the car or start in the car and in car, sleep behind the air-conditioning winter, all possibly cause gas poisoning. Because contain carbon monoxide 4%～8% in the vehicle exhaust, one 20 horsepowers motor car engine can produce 28 liters of carbon monoxide in one minute. (5) other are like the blasting fume that explosion under the mine produces, and chemical fertilizer factory uses coal gas to be raw material, and equipment failure, pipeline gas leakage etc. all can cause gas poisoning. Existing technology mainly is to go the improvement problem from the gas relief valve aspect, and a lot of technology are merely able to find, can not take precautions against, and not have a kind of active valid defensive measure that the coal gas that has leaked is effectively reduced concentration, avoid the generation of security incident. Find through retrieval; China's utility model patent: household fuel gas prevents escape protection device; Application number: 02290642.8 applying date: 2002-12-26, this utility model relates to a kind of coal gas and prevents escape protection device, " it is by reliever; air-sensitive circuit supervision device, and the control actuating unit is formed; It is characterized in that controlling the top that actuating unit is installed in reliever, air-sensitive circuit supervision device is connected through electromagnet with the control actuating unit." when though this utility model can be unmanned on the scene, close gas fitting automatically, under the situation that gas leak is arranged, can't active valid carry out effective concentration reduction to the coal gas that has leaked, avoid the generation of potential security incident. Summary of the invention The objective of the invention is to solve above-mentioned deficiency of the prior art; A kind of gas leak emergency protecting equipment is provided; When having detected gas leak and having reported to the police; Also adopt an effective measure the gas concentration that has leaked is reduced, thereby can further reduce the possibility that the gas leak security incident takes place, avoid unnecessary personnel's property loss. For realizing above-mentioned purpose, gas leak emergency protecting equipment of the present invention comprises: Sensor is connected with the PLC controller, is used to test airborne carbon monoxide content, and gives the PLC controller with measured data transfer; Alarm is connected with the PLC controller, is used for the warning of under unexpected situation, sounding; Fan is connected with the PLC controller, is used for the exhaust operation under unexpected situation; The PLC controller; The measurement parameter of its receiving sensor, if judging measurement parameter exceeds safety value, the control of PLC controller alarm, warning light and starting fan work; If judging measurement parameter is lower than safety value, then controls alarm, warning light and fan and quit work. Said sensor is that sensing part, PLC controller are that control section, alarm, warning light are the warning part, and fan is a mechanical part. Further, said sensor, PLC controller, warning light and alarm are arranged in the casing, connect through lead between them. Household fuel gas of the present invention leaks emergency protecting equipment to be merely able to find to prior art; Can not initiatively carry out the reduction of indoor gas content studies; In a single day when gas leak taking place, carry out effectively initiatively caution; And carry out active air draft effect, thereby the safety problem that active valid avoids the rising of indoor carbon monoxide content to cause through the driven by motor fan.The gas leak emergency protecting equipment of the present invention defensive measure of taking the initiative has the reliability height, can effectively avoid family gas to leak the safety problem that causes.
