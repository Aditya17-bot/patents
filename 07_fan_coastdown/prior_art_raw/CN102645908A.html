Abstract

The invention discloses a gas-leakage emergency protection device. The gas-leakage emergency protection device comprises a sensor, an alarm, an alarm lamp, a fan and a programmable logic controller (PLC), wherein the sensor is used for testing the content of carbon monoxide in air, and transmitting the tested data to the PLC; the alarm is used for giving an alarm in an emergency; the alarm lamp is used for cautioning in the emergency; the fan receives an instruction of the PLC, and discharges the indoor gas in an unexpected condition; and the PLC is used for controlling the start and shutdown of the sensor, the alarm, the alarm lamp and the fan. The gas-leakage emergency protection device disclosed by the invention adopts an active defense measure and has the advantages that the reliability is high and safety problems caused by household gas leakages can be effectively avoided.

Technical field
The present invention relates to a kind of gas protection device of civilian security fields, specifically, what relate to is a kind of gas leak emergency protecting equipment.
Background technology
Coal gas has accessed to most families, and the safety problem of coal gas aspect also is much accounted of day by day, estimates according to the expert; Family gas leaks; Can poison if airborne carbon monoxide content reaches 0.04%～0.06% o'clock people, reach at 12.5% o'clock, also possibly produce blast with air mixed.
The main cause of gas poisoning:
(1) in airtight room, use coal stove for heating, cook because improper ventilation, oxygen supply is insufficient, can produce a large amount of carbon monoxide savings indoor.
1. door and window closes, and does not have venting practice again, does not install or install incorrectly wind scoop.
2. careless and inadvertent, the thought paralysis causes coal gas to overflow in a large number.
3. the chimney installation is unreasonable, and nozzle flows backwards coal gas over against the air port.
4. weather conditions are bad, as blow, snow, cloudy day, air pressure be low, coal gas is difficult to circulation and discharges.
(2) the city resident uses piped gas, and its CO content is 25%～30%.If pipeline leaks gas, switch is not tight, or after flame was put out in hotting plate, coal gas overflowed in a large number, can cause poisoning.
(3) use gas heater, improper ventilation, bathing overlong time.
(4) in the garage, start up the car or start in the car and in car, sleep behind the air-conditioning winter, all possibly cause gas poisoning.
Because contain carbon monoxide 4%～8% in the vehicle exhaust, one 20 horsepowers motor car engine can produce 28 liters of carbon monoxide in one minute.
(5) other are like the blasting fume that explosion under the mine produces, and chemical fertilizer factory uses coal gas to be raw material, and equipment failure, pipeline gas leakage etc. all can cause gas poisoning.
Existing technology mainly is to go the improvement problem from the gas relief valve aspect, and a lot of technology are merely able to find, can not take precautions against, and not have a kind of active valid defensive measure that the coal gas that has leaked is effectively reduced concentration, avoid the generation of security incident.

The objective of the invention is to solve above-mentioned deficiency of the prior art; A kind of gas leak emergency protecting equipment is provided; When having detected gas leak and having reported to the police; Also adopt an effective measure the gas concentration that has leaked is reduced, thereby can further reduce the possibility that the gas leak security incident takes place, avoid unnecessary personnel's property loss.

Warning light 4 is connected with PLC controller 3, is used for issuing out optical signalling in unexpected situation.

In the present embodiment, said fan electromotor 1, PLC controller 3, warning light 4, alarm 5 and sensor 6 are settled like Fig. 1 position, connect through lead between them.

In the present embodiment, said sensor 6 is gas sensors, can be semi-conductor type gas sensor, galvanochemistry type gas sensor etc.
In the present embodiment; Sensor 6 is read airborne content of nitric oxide and is surpassed the Security alert value; It can send signal to PLC controller 3; And pipe and warning light 4 by PLC controller 3 control alarms 5, open fan electromotor 1 simultaneously and carry out in time air draft fast and effectively and reduce nitric oxide production content in the room air.
