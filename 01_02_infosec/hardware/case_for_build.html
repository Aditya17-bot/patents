The problem this solves

Every number in the disclosure today comes from a stand-in

The invention claims three things that separate it from the closest prior art (US20190156689A1, which flags a behavioural sequence and then suspends the exam in software): a question-onset anchor that is physically measured rather than read from a log; attribution of a sound to the seated candidate through structure-borne coupling, so a neighbour cannot implicate them; and an intervention that physically interrupts the keyboard's data path.

None of the three has been measured. The validation corpus supplied a software log marker instead of a photodiode, a pocket-worn phone instead of a desk sensor, and password typing instead of exam transcription. No public dataset carries synchronised photodiode, desk-contact and keystroke channels from one station — which is precisely why the parts have to exist.

A reviewer graded the current draft 3/10 on patent defensibility for exactly this reason. The reference filing that cleared stage 1 had hardware that was, in its own words, assembled and interconnected, with integration tests run against it.

Enforcement ladder

Nothing cuts a wire on one sensor's word

observe

Corroboration value below the eligibility threshold. No output of any kind.

advise

Desk indicator only. Requires persistence across consecutive control cycles, so one noisy cycle cannot actuate.

gate

Input path physically opened — and only when two physically independent channels each clear their own threshold.

hold

Session latched for invigilator review after repeated corroborated gating.

On replayed recordings the interlock cuts wrongful gating of unassisted candidates from 29.7 % to 10.8 % of questions, at a cost of a quarter of the true gates. The ladder as a whole holds 419 eligible cycles down to 83 actuations.

Stated honestly: that reduction is measured on top of a self-referential baseline, and the detector underneath it is modest — roughly 0.70–0.78 AUC on eleven candidates. Neither is fixed by building the node. They are fixed by collecting a corpus from the node once it exists.

Structure-borne coupling of whispered speech is the weakest link in the disclosure. If a candidate's own whisper does not separate from a neighbour's by a usable margin, the attribution unit needs re-framing around contact events — typing impulses, leaning, hand placement — and the claims should be rewritten before filing rather than after a rejection.

One afternoon with a piezo, a microphone and a willing second person answers it.

What the build unblocks

Three things, none of which more analysis can reach

  • Reduction to practice. The claimed transducers move from described to measured, matching the evidentiary position of the filing that already cleared stage 1.
  • A corpus of the actual system. Ten candidates, thirty minutes each, with photodiode, desk contact, gaze and keystrokes on one clock. That tests the invention rather than five stand-ins for it, and retires the eleven-candidate ceiling that no amount of statistics could lift.
  • An honest Section 8. Physical response times, sensor tolerances and actuation latencies measured on the apparatus — the form of evidence the examination guidelines ask for, and the form the current draft cannot supply.
