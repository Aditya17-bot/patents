# §4.1 and Claim 11 — rewrite required by the E3 measurements

Source of the requirement: `evidence/README.md`. Every number below is measured on SMD
group 3, 11 stations, 55 station pairs, 20 resampled trials, MIT-licensed corpus.

The headline for the drafting agent: **the refutation hands back a stronger
inventive-step argument than the one it destroys.** The disclosure currently argues that a
skilled person would not think to share one model across stations. The measurement says a
skilled person *would* share it — that is the obvious engineering instinct — and that
doing so **destroys** the coupling channel. An invention that teaches away from the
obvious choice, and gives the measured reason, is a better position than one that claims
the obvious choice as its insight.

---

## 1. What must be deleted

**§4.1 synergy paragraph, second and third sentences.** Currently:

> *"Because the present invention scores every station with one common normal-only model
> and one common label-free calibration procedure, the residual time-series of different
> stations are numerically comparable by construction. This makes available an entirely new
> detection channel …"*

Measured, on 11 held-out stations: one shared model **widens** residual scale disagreement
(4.1× spread of mean residual, against 2.8× for station-local models), transfers thresholds
worse (17.3 % of station pairs within 2× nominal FPR, against 22.7 %), and is **at chance**
at finding a coupled pair — AUROC 0.516–0.559, **zero detections in 20 trials**, against
AUROC 0.983 for station-local models. The stated causal claim is contradicted in direction.

**Claim 11, the shared-model clause.** Currently:

> *"…by means of **a single** self-supervised predictive model trained exclusively on
> sanctioned activity, **the model and a common calibration procedure being applied
> identically across said plurality of stations so that the residual time-series of
> different stations are expressed on a common scale**…"*

Three separate problems, any one of which is enough. It recites as a positive limitation
the thing the evidence refutes. Its *"so that"* clause is a functional assertion that is
measurably false, which is a sufficiency exposure under section 10(4) as well as an
inventive-step one. And it needlessly narrows the claim to a configuration that does not
work, while the configuration that does work — station-local models — falls outside it.

## 2. Replacement §4.1 synergy paragraph

> The synergy is specifically this. Element (e) is available only in combination with
> element (f), and is defeated rather than assisted by the arrangement a skilled person
> would be expected to adopt. Collusion is a relational phenomenon; every prior system
> listed in Section 3 scores candidates in isolation and is therefore structurally
> incapable of observing it, and a coupling computed between station residual streams is
> a channel that exists at no single station. That channel is not, however, available
> merely by computing it. Stations in a venue share an environment, so the most frequent
> cause of simultaneous residual elevation at neighbouring stations is not coordination but
> an event common to the room. Measured on eleven independently instrumented stations, a
> venue-wide event raises the mean pairwise coupling across all fifty-five station pairs
> from 0.061 to 0.334, and a genuinely coupled pair — although it then carries the highest
> raw coupling in the venue — is identified first out of fifty-five in only one trial in
> ten, because the venue-wide event lifts every pair with it. Estimating and subtracting the
> component common to the venue restores that identification to six trials in ten and raises
> the ranking accuracy from 0.632 to 0.983. Element (f) is therefore a precondition of the
> validity of element (e), not an optimisation of it.
>
> A second and less expected condition governs the same channel. The residual streams to be
> correlated must be generated by predictive models established **separately at each
> station**. Applying one model in common across the stations, which is the arrangement a
> person skilled in the art would naturally adopt for economy and for uniformity of scale,
> introduces into every stream the error peculiar to that one model, and that error is
> shared across the stations by construction. It therefore appears as coupling at every
> station pair and cannot be removed by a reference formed from the same stations, because
> it is itself part of what that reference measures. Measured, the effect is not marginal:
> with no coordinated activity present at all, forming the reference across stations scored
> by a common model raises mean pairwise coupling from 0.074 to 0.138, and the coupled pair
> is identified in none of twenty trials at any setting tested, the ranking accuracy
> remaining between 0.516 and 0.559 — that is, indistinguishable from chance. Station-local
> models under the same conditions reach 0.983. The arrangement claimed accordingly departs
> from the expedient a skilled person would adopt, and the departure is what makes the
> channel work.

Notes for the agent. Keep the numbers; they are the only measured support anything in the
cross-station claims has ever had. Do not soften *"indistinguishable from chance"* — it is
what 0.516–0.559 over twenty trials means, and understating it invites the examiner to
restate it more damagingly. Do not name Section 3(k) anywhere in this passage.

## 3. Replacement §4.1 element list

Elements (a), (b), (d) unchanged. Amend (c), (e), (f) and add (g):

> (c)  standardisation of that residual against statistics gathered from an initial
> sanctioned interval at the specific venue **and at the specific station**, which
> calibrates the detector to local ambient conditions without any labelled data;
>
> (e)  time-lagged correlation of residual time-series between spatially proximate
> stations, the residual time-series of each station being generated by a predictive model
> established at that station;
>
> (f)  suppression of a venue-wide common-mode residual component prior to that
> correlation, derived from a robust central statistic across the stations, from at least
> one instrumented but unoccupied reference station, or from both, so that the coordination
> condition responds only to coupling specific to a station pair;
>
> (g)  maintenance of timing alignment between the residual time-series of different
> stations within a tolerance bounded by the shortest lag at which the correlation is
> evaluated, without which a pair coupling and a venue-wide event are not separable.

Element (g) is new and is the audit's Tier-1 time-synchronisation item. It is no longer
asserted: measured on a coupling lag of two reporting intervals, ranking accuracy holds at
0.992, 0.987 and 0.981 for skews of zero, one half and one times the lag, falls to 0.954
at one and a half times, and to 0.726 at four times. Detection over the same range falls
0.75 → 0.60 → 0.35 → 0.15.

**§4.1(f) also needs the concession rewrite** drafted in `s3_additions.md` — common-mode
suppression is ordinary instrumentation practice and must be conceded as such before it is
distinguished, or the first instrumentation textbook answers it.

## 4. Amended Claim 11

Changes: the shared-model limitation and its purposive clause are deleted; models become
station-local; Claim 12's unoccupied reference station and Claim 15's synchronisation
arrangement are pulled up as mandatory elements, per the audit's Tier-1 plan; the timing
tolerance carries a number.

> **Claim 11 (independent).** A system for detecting coordinated malpractice between
> candidates, comprising: a plurality of monitored stations, each associated with a sensing
> assembly producing one or more signal streams; at least one further station disposed
> within the venue, instrumented with a like sensing assembly and held unoccupied during
> the monitored session; a time-synchronisation arrangement maintaining alignment between
> the signal streams of the said stations within a predetermined timing tolerance; and a
> processing arrangement configured to:
>
> generate, for each station, a prediction-residual time-series by means of a
> self-supervised predictive model trained exclusively on sanctioned activity, **the
> predictive model and its standardisation statistics being established separately for each
> said station from an interval of sanctioned activity at that station**;
>
> derive a common-mode component representing residual variation shared across the venue
> from a robust central statistic of the residual time-series of said plurality of stations
> **and from the residual time-series of the said unoccupied station**, and subtract said
> common-mode component from the residual time-series of each station;
>
> compute a time-lagged correlation statistic between the common-mode-corrected residual
> time-series of two or more spatially proximate stations, **over a set of lags of which the
> shortest is not less than twice the said timing tolerance**;
>
> indicate coordinated malpractice when said statistic satisfies a predetermined
> coordination condition; and
>
> indicate an isolated individual anomaly when an elevated residual at one station is
> unaccompanied by a correlated elevation at a proximate station.

**Consequential amendments.**

- **Claim 12** loses the unoccupied reference station to Claim 11. Repurpose it to the
  robust statistic itself: *"wherein the robust central statistic is a median taken across
  the said plurality of stations at each sampling instant"* — measured as the best of the
  three reference rules tested (0.983, against 0.920 for a per-station-standardised median
  and 0.632 for no reference at all).
- **Claim 15** loses the synchronisation arrangement to Claim 11. Repurpose it to the
  narrower tolerance: *"wherein the said timing tolerance does not exceed one half of the
  shortest lag at which the correlation statistic is evaluated"* — the setting at which
  ranking accuracy measured 0.987.
- **New dependent, on the exchange count.** *"…wherein the coordination condition requires
  the correlation statistic to be satisfied over at least a predetermined number of
  separate coupled events within the monitored session."* Supported: ranking accuracy 0.759
  at ten events, 0.852 at twenty, 0.929 at forty, 0.992 at ninety. This claim also protects
  the specification, because the channel is a correlation estimate and **a single exchange
  is not detectable by it**. The document must not imply otherwise anywhere.
- **Claim 1** retains the shared-model language only if it is wanted for the single-station
  case, where none of the above applies. It is cleaner to align it: replace *"trained
  exclusively on … containing no labelled instance of malpractice"* with a
  runtime-inspectable limitation, which is the audit's Tier-2 item on product-by-process.

## 5. What this does not fix

Nothing here touches **Section 3(k)**. Claim 11 still ends in *"indicate"*. The amendment
adds one physical element — the instrumented unoccupied station — which is a real
transducer with no counterpart in GDN, CST-GL or any assessment-integrity art, and that is
worth having. It is not by itself a physical actuation. The audit's finding stands: the
Label-Free disclosure has no equivalent of the CausalInterlock disclosure opening the
keyboard data lines, and no measurement changes that.

Nothing here re-derives **§8's simulated cross-station figures** (acoustic 0.468, EM 0.739,
behavioural 0.650, combined 0.810). They were generated under the shared-model assumption
that has just been refuted. They should be withdrawn or re-derived before filing, not
quoted alongside the new measurements.
