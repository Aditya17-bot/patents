## Consequence for claims

The broad idea "detect field capacity from the response to watering and set targets relative to it" is
known (US 7,133,749). Claim core must include at least:
1. continuous (rolling, causal) re-anchoring from routine production pulses, with drain onset detected as
   collapse of the per-pulse WC rise relative to that day's pre-drain rise, and
2. mapping each of several sensors on one substrate type to a common substrate-type plateau constant
   (cross-sensor consistency), then valve/dosing actuation on the corrected reading.
Dependents: two-route relative gain with agreement gate; gain-free delivery ratio between slabs with drift
alarm; dry-back steering on the corrected reading; common constant from the median of sensor plateaus.
