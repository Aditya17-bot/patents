CN118914491A - Water quality monitoring station calibration, compensation, data processing and
evaluation method and system. Chengdu Borui Kechuan Technology Co ltd; priority 2024-08-12;
published 2024-11-08; status pending.

Abstract: "The method includes: systematically calibrating a water quality monitoring station;
performing environmental compensation on a water quality monitoring station; processing
monitoring data acquired by a water quality monitoring station; and evaluating water quality
using continuous monitoring data acquired by a water quality monitoring station."

Summary: "Environmental compensation for water quality monitoring stations, including: sludge
impact compensation for ion selective sensors; microbial impact compensation for UVCOD sensors;
installation position compensation for sensors"

Sludge compensation: "The monitoring value C v of the ion selective sensor is corrected by using
the influence value ΔC sum of the sludge covered by the current sensor installation cabin on the
ion selective sensor to obtain the corrected monitoring value C w" - the sludge amount is
modelled from time since maintenance, water velocity, sludge concentration and viscosity.

Smoothing: "Smoothing the monitoring data after calibration using concentrated water samples
within a calibration period" with a rate of change and offset interpolated between the
concentrated-water-sample calibration slope/offset of the current and the last calibration cycle.

Not found in the text read: conductivity used to estimate interfering-ion activity; any
correction of the ion-selective reading proportional to a co-measured conductivity.

CN111398549A - A reagent-free multi-parameter water quality in-situ monitor. Yangtze Delta Region
Institute of Tsinghua University Zhejiang; priority 2020-04-13.

Abstract: "The electrode method module and the photometric method module are used to correspond
to temperature, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, pH, ammonia nitrogen turbidity And chemical
oxygen demand and other testing items, no need to add any reagents, effectively monitor water
quality changes in real time, and give warnings to emergencies."
Hardware (buoy platform, detection system, solar panels); no interference correction described
in the abstract.
