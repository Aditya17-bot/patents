US 5,594,667 A - Method and apparatus for baseline drift correction for a sensor (Allen Myers;
priority 1992-07-10; granted 1997-01-14; expired).
Abstract (excerpt): "In one embodiment of the invention, baseline level is calculated as the average of
a fixed percentage of the points in the data set which have the lowest values found in the data set."
Applied to a grain mass flow rate sensor in a harvester (cyclically varying parameter); the baseline
is the sensor's zero, subtracted continually. CLOSEST to the floor-tracking idea: a low-quantile
baseline of the sensor's own record. Differences: no reference (laboratory) measurement anchors the
baseline; the baseline is taken as zero signal, not as a non-zero, site-specific true background.

US 2019/0034032 A1 (granted as US 11,294,503 B2) - Sensor baseline offset adjustment (Apple Inc).
Touch-sensor panel: delays baseline updates while ungrounded objects are present; global baseline
offset for temperature changes. Not relevant to water-quality sensors.

US 6,237,394 B1 - Apparatus and method for correcting drift in a sensor (Redwood Microsystems / SMC).
