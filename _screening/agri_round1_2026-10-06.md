## NEON STER (dryland wheat) soil water, 2023 Apr-Sep (token working 2026-10-06)
- 5 plots x 8 depths (0.02-1.36 m), EnviroSCAN-type capacitance; NEON's own expanded uncertainty 0.214 m3/m3 on every sensor.
- Only 0-87 % of readings pass QF (median ~0.6); two sensors 0 %. Medians implausible and non-monotone
  (e.g. 0.47 at 0.16 m next to 0.25 at 0.26 m; 0.10 at 1.36 m) -> per-sensor scaling errors are large (gate 2 passes).
- Rain probe (09_substrate_wc/results/probe_rain_storage.json): storage gain to 0.66 m = 0.1-0.4 of tipping-bucket rain
  (7 usable plot-events, mostly plot 2). Too few to separate runoff/evaporation from sensor gain.
- Gate 4 concern: two-point rescaling of uncalibrated probes from 'full point' (post-drainage plateau) and 'refill point'
  is standard irrigation-industry practice (Sentek/AquaCheck); SM2RAIN covers rain-mass-balance. Needs a sharper mechanism.
- Night-conservation probe (09_substrate_wc/results/probe_night_conservation.json): fit gives negative, unstable gains
  (e.g. plot 5 halves -1.46 vs +3.41). Night changes 0.3-1.8 mm, dominated by sensor temperature artefacts, not
  redistribution. FAILED at gate 5.
